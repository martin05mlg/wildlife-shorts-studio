Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Wildlife Shorts Studio respects your privacy. All video analysis, cropping, and encoding run locally on your Windows PC.

1. Information We Collect

We do not operate any external backend server and we do not collect, track, sell, or share personal data, telemetry, or analytics.

2. OAuth Tokens & API Access

When you connect your own YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram account via OAuth 2.0, the application only requests the permissions required to upload videos you explicitly approve (for example, youtube.upload or TikTok video upload scopes).

  • Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored exclusively on your local hard drive inside your local configuration folder.
  • Tokens are sent directly from your computer over HTTPS to the official Google, TikTok, or Meta API endpoints solely to perform user-initiated uploads or refresh expired tokens.

3. Third-Party Privacy Policies

API requests made by your authorized sessions are subject to the Google Privacy Policy and the respective TikTok and Meta privacy policies. You can revoke access at any time from your Google, TikTok, or Instagram security settings, or by clicking Disconnect inside the application.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete all stored credentials and local databases at any time by removing your local configuration files or using the application cleanup options.

5. Contact

If you have any privacy questions or deletion requests, contact: martin05mlg@gmail.com.
