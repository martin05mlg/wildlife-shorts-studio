Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Wildlife Shorts Studio respects your privacy. All video analysis, cropping, and encoding run locally on your Windows PC.

1. Information We Collect

We do not operate any external backend server and we do not collect, track, sell, or share personal data, telemetry, or analytics.

2. Google User Data & YouTube API Services

2.1. Google User Data Accessed

Wildlife Shorts Studio requests only the OAuth 2.0 scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. This permission is used solely to upload user-approved videos along with user-configured metadata (title, description, tags, and privacy status) to the authenticated user's YouTube channel. The application does not read, list, modify, or delete existing YouTube videos, playlists, comments, subscriptions, or personal Google profile information.

2.2. Data Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure

Wildlife Shorts Studio does not sell, share, transfer, or disclose Google user data or OAuth tokens to any third parties, advertisers, or external servers. All API requests occur directly between the local desktop application on the user's device and official Google/YouTube API endpoints. Wildlife Shorts Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2.3. Data Protection Mechanisms

Authentication uses OAuth 2.0 with PKCE (S256) over HTTPS and a local loopback redirect (127.0.0.1). OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored exclusively on your local hard drive inside your local configuration folder and are never transmitted to any developer-controlled or third-party server.

2.4. Data Retention and Deletion

OAuth tokens and local configuration data are retained locally only for as long as you keep your account connected to the application. You can disconnect your account and delete all stored credentials and local databases at any time from within the application or by removing your local configuration files. You can also revoke Google access directly at any time via your Google Account Permissions page, which immediately terminates all API access. No user data is retained on external servers.

3. Other Platform Integrations & Third-Party Policies

When you connect your own TikTok or Instagram account via OAuth 2.0, tokens are likewise stored exclusively on your local machine and sent only over HTTPS to official platform endpoints for user-initiated uploads. API requests made by your authorized sessions are subject to the Google Privacy Policy and the respective TikTok and Meta privacy policies.
